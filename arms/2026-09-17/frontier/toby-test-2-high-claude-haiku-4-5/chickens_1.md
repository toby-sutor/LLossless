How to take care of backyard chickens
Keep at least 3 birds, as chickens are social animals and get easily stressed when alone. Provide roughly 4 sq ft per chicken inside the coop plus around 10 sq ft each in the bigger run.

Predator-proofing is the most imoprtant aspect for safety: use robust chicken wire, bury the fence in to the ground around the perimeter against foxes, and use an automatic door that closes at dusk.

Provide the chickens with designated chicken feed that contains important vitamins and minerals. Provide clean water available 24/7. Clean the waterers often and thouroughly to stop algae and mould.

Add some absorbent bedding like pine shavings (around 4-6 inches), and replace it weekly, top it up as needed. Offer 1 nesting box per 3/4 hens and 8-12 inches of roosting bar per bird will help them feel safe and comfy.

Finally, it is important to check the flock daily so you understand what "normal" behavior looks like for each hen, quarantine any new birds for 7-10 days before introduction to avoid spread of illness and parasites, and compost manure before it goes anywhere near the vegetable beds.
