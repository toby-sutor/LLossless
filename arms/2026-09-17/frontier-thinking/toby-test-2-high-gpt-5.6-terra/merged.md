How to take care of backyard chickens

Keep at least 3 birds because chickens are social animals and get easily stressed when alone. Provide roughly 4 sq ft per chicken inside the coop and around 10 sq ft each in the bigger run.

Predator-proofing is the most important aspect of safety: use ¼" galvanised hardware cloth rather than chicken wire, bury a 12-inch apron around the perimeter against diggers, and fit two-step latches or an automatic door that closes at dusk.

Provide chickens with designated chicken feed containing important vitamins and minerals, using a complete layer ration supplemented by whatever they forage. Keep clean water available 24/7, and empty and scrub waterers often and thoroughly to stop algae and mould.

Provide 4-6 inches of absorbent bedding, such as pine shavings or similar; stir it weekly, top it up as needed, and strip it out every 6-12 months. Provide 1 nesting box per 3/4 hens and 8-12 inches of roosting bar per bird to help chickens feel safe and comfy while keeping things orderly.

Check the flock daily so you understand what "normal" behavior looks like for each hen. Quarantine any new birds for 7-10 days before introduction to avoid the spread of illness and parasites, and compost manure before it goes anywhere near the vegetable beds.
