Vegetarian Thai Curry Receipt

Serves 4 · ~30 min

Ingredients

- 1 tbsp coconut oil
- 2-3 tbsp green curry paste (check it's veg - many contain shrimp paste)
- 1 can (400 ml) full-fat coconut milk
- 1 onion, sliced
- 2 cups mixed vegetables: bell pepper, green beans, baby corn, broccoli, mushrooms, eggplant
- 200 g firm tofu, cubed (optional)
- 1 tsp palm or brown sugar
- 1-2 tbsp soy sauce or 1 tsp salt
- Juice of ½ lime
- Handful Thai basil (or regular basil)
- Jasmine rice, to serve
- 400g extra-firm tofu, cubed
- 2–4 tbsp green curry paste (adjust to taste)
- 1 cup broccoli florets
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 cup zucchini, sliced
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 4–6 kaffir lime leaves, lightly crushed
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil

Method

1. Spoon the thick cream from the top of the coconut milk into a hot wok with the oil.
   Cook until it bubbles and the oil separates, ~2 min.
2. Add the curry paste and fry 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add onion and the remaining coconut milk.
   Bring to a gentle simmer - don't boil hard.
4. Add the harder vegetables first (beans, broccoli, eggplant), simmer 5 min, then the softer ones (pepper, mushrooms) and the tofu.
   Simmer another 5 min.
5. Season with sugar, soy sauce and lime juice.
   Taste and adjust - it should be salty, slightly sweet, and sour.
6. Stir in the basil off the heat.
   Serve over jasmine rice.

Tip: Add a splash of water or veg stock if it gets too thick.

1. Press & fry the tofu.
   Press excess water from the tofu, cut into cubes, and pan-fry in a little oil until golden on all sides.
   Set aside.

2. Bloom the paste.
   Heat oil in a wok or large pan over medium heat.
   Add the curry paste and stir for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.

3. Add coconut milk.
   Pour in half the coconut milk and stir until the paste is fully dissolved, then add the rest along with the soy sauce, brown sugar, and lime leaves.

4. Cook the vegetables.
   Bring to a gentle simmer, add the broccoli, bell pepper, and zucchini.
   Cook for 4–5 minutes until tender but still crisp.

5. Finish.
   Add the tofu, lime juice, and Thai basil.
   Stir, taste, and adjust seasoning with salt or more soy sauce.

6. Serve over steamed jasmine rice with extra basil and a wedge of lime.

Tips: For a richer curry, use a splash of vegetable stock.
Swap in any seasonal vegetables you like — baby corn, sugar snap peas, and mushrooms all work great.
