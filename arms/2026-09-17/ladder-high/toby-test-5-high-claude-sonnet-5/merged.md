Vegetarian Thai Curry Receipt

Serves 4 · ~30 min

Ingredients

- 1 tbsp coconut oil
- 2–4 tbsp green curry paste, adjust to taste (check it's vegetarian - many contain shrimp paste)
- 1 can (400 ml) full-fat coconut milk
- 1 onion, sliced
- 200 g firm tofu, cubed (optional)
- 4–6 kaffir lime leaves, lightly crushed
- 2 cups mixed vegetables: bell pepper, green beans, baby corn, broccoli, zucchini, mushrooms, eggplant
- 1 tsp palm or brown sugar
- 1-2 tbsp soy sauce or 1 tsp salt
- Juice of ½ lime
- Handful Thai basil (or regular basil)
- Jasmine rice, to serve

Method

1. If using tofu, press out excess water, cut it into cubes, and pan-fry in a little oil until golden on all sides; set aside.
2. Spoon the thick cream from the top of the coconut milk into a hot wok with the coconut oil. Cook until it bubbles and the oil separates, ~2 min.
3. Add the curry paste and fry for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.
4. Add the onion, then stir in the remaining coconut milk - pouring in half first and stirring until the paste dissolves before adding the rest along with the soy sauce (or salt), sugar, and kaffir lime leaves. Bring to a gentle simmer - don't boil hard.
5. Add the harder vegetables first (green beans, broccoli, eggplant) and simmer 5 min, then add the softer vegetables (bell pepper, mushrooms, zucchini) and simmer for a further 4–5 minutes until tender but still crisp.
6. Stir in the tofu, lime juice and Thai basil off the heat. Taste and adjust the seasoning with more soy sauce or salt - it should be salty, slightly sweet, and sour.
7. Serve over steamed jasmine rice, with extra basil and a wedge of lime.

Tip: Add a splash of water or vegetable stock if the curry gets too thick, or for a richer flavour. Feel free to swap in any seasonal vegetables you like — baby corn, sugar snap peas, and mushrooms all work great.
