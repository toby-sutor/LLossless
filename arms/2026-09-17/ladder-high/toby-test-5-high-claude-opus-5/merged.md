Vegetarian Thai Curry Receipt

Serves 4 · ~30 min

Ingredients

- 1 tbsp coconut oil
- 2-3 tbsp green curry paste, adjusted to taste (check it's veg - many contain shrimp paste)
- 1 can (400 ml) full-fat coconut milk
- 1 onion, sliced
- 2 cups mixed vegetables: red bell pepper, green beans, baby corn, broccoli, mushrooms, eggplant, zucchini
- 200 g firm tofu, cubed (optional)
- 1 tsp palm or brown sugar
- 1-2 tbsp soy sauce or 1 tsp salt
- Juice of ½ lime
- 4–6 kaffir lime leaves, lightly crushed
- Handful Thai basil (or regular basil)
- Jasmine rice, to serve

Method

1. Press excess water from the tofu, cut into cubes, and pan-fry in a little oil until golden on all sides.
   Set aside.
2. Spoon the thick cream from the top of the coconut milk into a hot wok or large pan with the oil.
   Cook until it bubbles and the oil separates, ~2 min.
3. Add the curry paste and stir for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.
4. Add the onion, the kaffir lime leaves and the remaining coconut milk, stirring until the paste is fully dissolved.
   Bring to a gentle simmer - don't boil hard.
5. Add the harder vegetables first (beans, broccoli, eggplant), simmer 5 min, then the softer ones (pepper, mushrooms) and the tofu.
   Simmer another 5 min, until the vegetables are tender but still crisp.
6. Season with sugar, soy sauce and lime juice.
   Taste and adjust with salt or more soy sauce - it should be salty, slightly sweet, and sour.
7. Stir in the basil off the heat.
   Serve over steamed jasmine rice with extra basil and a wedge of lime.

Tips: Add a splash of water or veg stock if it gets too thick; a splash of vegetable stock also makes for a richer curry.
Swap in any seasonal vegetables you like — baby corn, sugar snap peas, and mushrooms all work great.
