# Dock Fault Escalation - Field Crew

## What riders report
Riders call about bikes that will not release and bikes that will not lock, and both usually indicate a dock fault rather than a bike fault.

## Fault codes on the dock display
A dock showing E2 has a jammed locking pin, and one showing E4 has lost power to the point; the support desk asks the rider to read out the code on the dock display. E2 is a mechanical fault and E4 is an electrical one, and they are escalated differently.

## Clearing E2 in the field
Free the pin with the service key and cycle the dock twice. If the dock takes a bike and releases it on both cycles, close the fault on your handheld.

## When to escalate E2
The support desk raises an E2 to the field crew. Escalate E2 to the workshop after 2 failed attempts: do not keep cycling a dock that has already failed twice, since repeated forcing bends the pin and turns a 20 minute workshop job into a replacement.

## E4 is never a field fix
Report E4 to the electrical contractor the same day. Field crew do not open the power enclosure under any circumstances.

## Refunds
A rider charged for a journey that ended at a faulted dock gets the journey refunded. Refund at the time of the call; do not wait for the fault to be confirmed.

## Taking a station out of service
When more than half of a station's docks are faulted, the support desk takes the station out of service in the app. Field crew must tell the support desk before this happens, not after.
