# Hold Shelf Management

## Placing an item on the shelf

You can reserve an item that is on loan or held at another branch. We email you when it is ready to collect. Bring your library card. Print a hold slip and fold it into the item so the name is visible from the spine. Shelve items alphabetically by the borrower's surname. Items are on the hold shelf under your surname.

## How long an item waits

An item waits on the hold shelf for 7 days. The 7-day period starts on the day the borrower is notified by email, not the day the item arrives.

## Clearing the shelf

Sweep the shelf every morning before opening. Items past 7 days go back into circulation, or on to the next borrower in the queue if there is one.

## Borrowers who cannot collect in time

A borrower who asks before the 7 days are up can have the item held for a further 7 days. This is granted once per request.

## Items nobody collects twice

If an item has been on the hold shelf twice without being collected for the same reservation, it goes back to the shelves and the reservation is cancelled; the borrower must reserve it again.
