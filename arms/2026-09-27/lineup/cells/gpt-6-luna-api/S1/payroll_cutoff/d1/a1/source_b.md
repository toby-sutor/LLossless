# Payroll Submission Deadlines (2025 Schedule)

## Who this is for

Team leads who submit timesheet totals for hourly staff.

## Monthly cut-off

Totals must be submitted by 15:00 on the 20th of the month. Submissions that
arrive after the cut-off are held for the following month's run.

If the 20th falls on a weekend, the cut-off moves to the following Monday.

## What to submit

Submit one file per team listing every hourly worker with hours and the cost
centre the hours are charged to.

## Corrections

A correction submitted before the cut-off replaces the earlier file entirely.
After the cut-off, corrections go to the payroll inbox as a written request.

## Paper timesheets

Teams without terminal access may send paper timesheets to the payroll office by
internal post, to arrive no later than the cut-off.
