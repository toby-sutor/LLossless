# The pretty Rosegarden

Once upon a time, there was a princess in a distant country that loved to spend time in her beautiful rosegarden.

## Times

She visited the rosegarden every morning from 8 am till 9 am, right after her breakfast.
From 02:00 till 15:30 o'clock, she worked in the rosegarden to tidy it up. Her mother did not appreciate that she, as a princess, would actually work in the garden, but the princess didn't care.

## Winter

The rosegarden lost all his life in winter, so the young lady could not enjoy the beautiful colors and all the life the rosegarden harbored.
But once spring arrived, it turned back to life and color.

## Public times

While the rose garden was the private property of the royal family, the young princess insisted on making the garden accessible to the public every March 1st to March 31st.
During that time, people from all over the country traveled to the castel to enjoy a nice day wandering through the rosegarden just like their beloved princess.

## Postcards

It became tradition that everyone who visited the rosegarden from the outside picked their favorite scenery and tried to draw what they liked most. They then sent the postcard to their home.
People were starting to make bets on whether the postcard arrived at their home before they arrived home.
In many cases, it was a tie because the people and the postcards were usually transported in the same carriage.

# The creepy forest

Once upon a time there was an old lady that lived in a dark, spooky forest.

## Seasons

The forest was not traversable between October and April as it was too wet and too cold.
The old lady nevertheless lived the entire year in the forest. Over the years, she acclimated to the weather and built strategies to survive the harsh conditions.

## Work

While many people suspected the old lady would be a witch that worked on forbidden magic, the reality was less exciting. Instead of harvesting skulls and bones, she collected wild herbs.
Those wild herbs were tried and sold to wandering travelers who transported and sold the herbs into the cities.

## Illness

In one harsh winter, many people of the nearby village became very sick. In a desperate attempt to help the villagers, one young man took the courage and traveled to the old lady's house deep in the woods.
It took him days to reach her house as he struggled with the wet and slippery ground. He also lost his orientation several times.
But eventually, he reached her house. He carefully knocked and hoped that all the rumors about the lady were untrue.
When she opened the door and saw the young man, she asked him to come into her house, sit at the fire and drink some hot soup she made for him.

## End

When the man recovered again, she gave him enough medicine made out of the herbs she collected in spring for the entire village.
He traveled back to his village and went to the home of every sick person to provide them with the correct amount of medicine as told by the old lady from the woods.
Eventually, everyone got healthy again and from then on the villagers sent a huge basket of cheese and dried meat to the old lady every autumn so that she could feast in winter.
