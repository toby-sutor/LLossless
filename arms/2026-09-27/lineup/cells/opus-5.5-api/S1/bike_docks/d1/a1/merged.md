# Dock Fault Escalation - Field Crew

## What riders report

Riders call about bikes that will not release and bikes that will not lock. Both usually mean a dock fault rather than a bike fault.

## Fault codes on the dock display

A dock showing E2 has a jammed locking pin. A dock showing E4 has lost power to the point. E2 is a mechanical fault and E4 is an electrical one, so the two are escalated differently. When a rider calls, the support desk asks the rider to read the code on the dock display.

## Clearing E2 in the field

Free the pin with the service key and cycle the dock twice. If the dock takes a bike and releases it on both cycles, close the fault on your handheld.

## When to escalate E2

The support desk raises an E2 to the field crew. Escalate E2 to the workshop after 2 failed attempts. Do not keep cycling a dock that has failed twice; repeated forcing bends the pin and turns a 20 minute workshop job into a replacement.

## E4 is never a field fix

Report E4 to the electrical contractor the same day. Field crew do not open the power enclosure under any circumstances.

## Refunds

A rider charged for a journey that ended at a faulted dock gets the journey refunded. Refund at the time of the call; do not wait for the fault to be confirmed.

## Taking a station out of service

A station with more than half its docks faulted is taken out of service in the app. Tell the support desk before you do this, not after.
