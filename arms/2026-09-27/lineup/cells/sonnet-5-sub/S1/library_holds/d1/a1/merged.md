# Hold Shelf Management

## Placing an item on the shelf

You can reserve an item that is on loan or held at another branch. When a requested item arrives, print a hold slip and fold it into the item so the name is visible from the spine, and shelve it alphabetically by the borrower's surname. Bring your library card when you come to collect it.

## How long an item waits

An item waits on the hold shelf for 7 days. Day one is the day we email the borrower to let them know it is ready to collect, not the day the item arrived.

## Clearing the shelf

Sweep the shelf every morning before opening. Items past 7 days go back into circulation, or on to the next borrower in the queue if there is one.

## Borrowers who cannot collect in time

A borrower who asks before the 7 days are up can have the item held for a further 7 days. This is granted once per request.

## Items nobody collects twice

An item that has been on the hold shelf twice without being collected goes back to the shelves and the request is cancelled; the borrower will need to reserve it again.
