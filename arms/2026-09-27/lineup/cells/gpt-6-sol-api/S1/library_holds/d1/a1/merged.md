# Hold Shelf Management

## Placing an item on the shelf

Items on loan or held at another branch can be reserved. When a requested item arrives, print a hold slip and fold it into the item so the borrower's name is visible from the spine. Shelve items alphabetically by the borrower's surname. Email the borrower when the item is ready to collect. Borrowers bring their library card to collect an item.

## How long an item waits

An item waits on the hold shelf for 7 days. Day one is the day the borrower is notified by email, not the day the item arrived.

## Clearing the shelf

Sweep the shelf every morning before opening. Items past 7 days go back into circulation, or on to the next borrower in the queue if there is one.

## Borrowers who cannot collect in time

A borrower who asks before the 7 days are up can have the item held for a further 7 days. This is granted once per request.

## Items nobody collects twice

An item that has been on the hold shelf twice without being collected under the same request goes back to the shelves and the request is cancelled. The borrower needs to reserve it again.
