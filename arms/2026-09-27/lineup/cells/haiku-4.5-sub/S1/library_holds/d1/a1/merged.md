# Hold Shelf Management

## Reservations

Borrowers can reserve items that are on loan or held at another branch. When the item is ready for collection, the borrower is notified by email.

## Placing an item on the hold shelf

When a reserved item arrives, print a hold slip and fold it into the item so the name is visible from the spine. Shelve items alphabetically by the borrower's surname.

## How long items are held

A reserved item waits on the hold shelf for 7 days from when the borrower is notified—day one is the notification day, not the arrival day.

## Clearing the shelf

Sweep the shelf every morning before opening. Items not collected within 7 days go back into circulation or move to the next borrower in the queue if there is one.

## Extending holds

If a borrower cannot collect within 7 days, they can request a 7-day extension before the deadline expires. This extension is granted once per reservation.

## Uncollected items

If an item remains uncollected after being held twice, it goes back to the shelves and the reservation is cancelled. The borrower must place a new reservation if they wish to request the item again.
