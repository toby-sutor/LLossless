# Hold Shelf Management

## Placing an item on the shelf

You can reserve an item that is on loan or held at another branch, and we will email you when it is ready to collect. When a requested item arrives, print a hold slip and fold it into the item so the name is visible from the spine. Shelve items alphabetically by the borrower's surname.

## How long an item waits

Bring your library card. Items are on the hold shelf under your surname. An item waits on the hold shelf for 7 days. Day one is the day the borrower is notified, not the day the item arrived.

## Clearing the shelf

Sweep the shelf every morning before opening. Items past 7 days go back into circulation, or on to the next borrower in the queue if there is one.

## Borrowers who cannot collect in time

A borrower who asks before the 7 days are up can have the item held for a further 7 days. This is granted once per request.

## Items nobody collects twice

An item that has been on the hold shelf twice without being collected goes back to the shelves and the request is cancelled, and you will need to reserve it again.
