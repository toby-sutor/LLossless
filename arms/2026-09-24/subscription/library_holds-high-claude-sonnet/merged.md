# Hold Shelf Management

## Reserving an item

You can reserve an item that is on loan or held at another branch. We will email you when it is ready to collect.

## Placing an item on the shelf

When a requested item arrives, print a hold slip and fold it into the item so the name is visible from the spine. Shelve items alphabetically by the borrower's surname; this is also where borrowers will find their item, so remind them to bring a library card when collecting.

## How long an item waits

An item waits on the hold shelf for 7 days. Day one is the day the borrower is notified by email, not the day the item arrived.

## Clearing the shelf

Sweep the shelf every morning before opening. Items past 7 days go back into circulation, or on to the next borrower in the queue if there is one.

## Borrowers who cannot collect in time

A borrower who asks before the 7 days are up can have the item held for a further 7 days. This is granted once per request.

## Items nobody collects twice

An item that has been on the hold shelf twice without being collected goes back to the shelves and the request is cancelled; the borrower will need to submit a new request if they still want it.
