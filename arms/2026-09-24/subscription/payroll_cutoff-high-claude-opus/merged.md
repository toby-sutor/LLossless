# Payroll Submission Deadlines (2026 Schedule)

## Who this is for

Team leads who submit timesheet totals for hourly staff. Salaried staff are not covered by this page.

## Monthly cut-off

Totals must be submitted by 12:00 on the 18th of the month. The 18th is a hard cut-off, not a target: submissions that arrive after it are held for the following month's run.

If the 18th falls on a weekend or a public holiday, the cut-off moves to the last working day before it.

## What to submit

Submit one file per team, listing every hourly worker with hours and the cost centre the hours are charged to. Blank rows are rejected by the upload page.

## Corrections

A correction submitted before the cut-off replaces the earlier file entirely. After the cut-off, corrections go to the payroll inbox as a written request and are applied to the following month.

## Paper timesheets

Teams without terminal access may send paper timesheets to the payroll office by internal post, to arrive no later than the cut-off.

## Escalation

If the upload page is unavailable in the hour before the cut-off, email the payroll team and keep the file. Do not send totals in the body of an email.
