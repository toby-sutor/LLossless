# Hold Shelf Management

Borrowers can reserve items that are on loan or held at another branch. When a reserved item is ready, we email the borrower.

## Placing an item on the shelf

When a requested item arrives, print a hold slip and fold it into the item so the name is visible from the spine. Shelve items alphabetically by the borrower's surname. Borrowers should bring their library card to collect items, which are located on the hold shelf under their surname.

## How long an item waits

An item waits on the hold shelf for 7 days from the day the borrower is notified.

## Clearing the shelf

Sweep the shelf every morning before opening. Items past 7 days go back into circulation, or on to the next borrower in the queue if there is one.

## Borrowers who cannot collect in time

A borrower who asks before the 7 days are up can have the item held for a further 7 days. This is granted once per request.

## Items nobody collects twice

An item that is not collected goes back into circulation or to the next borrower in the queue. If an item has been on the hold shelf twice without being collected, the request is cancelled.
