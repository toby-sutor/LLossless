Vegetarian Thai Curry Receipt

Serves 4 · ~30 min

Ingredients

- 1 tbsp coconut oil
- 2-3 tbsp green curry paste (check it's veg - many contain shrimp paste)
- 1 can (400 ml) full-fat coconut milk
- 1 onion, sliced
- 2 cups mixed vegetables: bell pepper, green beans, baby corn, broccoli, mushrooms, eggplant
- 200 g firm tofu, cubed (optional)
- 1 tsp palm or brown sugar
- 1-2 tbsp soy sauce or 1 tsp salt
- Juice of ½ lime
- Handful Thai basil (or regular basil)
- Jasmine rice, to serve

Method

1. Spoon the thick cream from the top of the coconut milk into a hot wok with the oil.
   Cook until it bubbles and the oil separates, ~2 min.
2. Add the curry paste and fry 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add onion and the remaining coconut milk.
   Bring to a gentle simmer - don't boil hard.
4. Add the harder vegetables first (beans, broccoli, eggplant), simmer 5 min, then the softer ones (pepper, mushrooms) and the tofu.
   Simmer another 5 min.
5. Season with sugar, soy sauce and lime juice.
   Taste and adjust - it should be salty, slightly sweet, and sour.
6. Stir in the basil off the heat.
   Serve over jasmine rice.

Tip: Add a splash of water or veg stock if it gets too thick.
