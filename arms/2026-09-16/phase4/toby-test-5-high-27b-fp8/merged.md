Vegetarian Thai Curry Receipt

Serves 4 · ~30 min

Ingredients

- 1 tbsp coconut oil
- 2-3 tbsp green curry paste (check it's veg - many contain shrimp paste)
- 1 can (400 ml) full-fat coconut milk
- 1 onion, sliced
- 2 cups mixed vegetables: bell pepper, green beans, baby corn, broccoli, mushrooms, eggplant, zucchini
- 200 g firm tofu, cubed (optional)
- 1 tsp palm or brown sugar
- 1-2 tbsp soy sauce or 1 tsp salt
- Juice of ½ lime
- Handful Thai basil (or regular basil)
- 4–6 kaffir lime leaves, lightly crushed
- Jasmine rice, to serve

Method

1. Press and fry the tofu. Press excess water from the tofu, cut into cubes, and pan-fry in a little oil until golden on all sides. Set aside.
2. Bloom the paste. Heat oil in a wok or large pan over medium heat. Add the curry paste and stir for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.
3. Add coconut milk. Pour in half the coconut milk and stir until the paste is fully dissolved, then add the rest along with the soy sauce, brown sugar, and lime leaves.
4. Cook the vegetables. Bring to a gentle simmer, add the broccoli, bell pepper, and zucchini. Cook for 4–5 minutes until tender but still crisp.
5. Finish. Add the tofu, lime juice, and Thai basil. Stir, taste, and adjust seasoning with salt or more soy sauce.
6. Serve over steamed jasmine rice with extra basil and a wedge of lime.

Tip: Add a splash of water or veg stock if it gets too thick. For a richer curry, use a splash of vegetable stock. Swap in any seasonal vegetables you like — baby corn, sugar snap peas, and mushrooms all work great.
