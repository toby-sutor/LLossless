# Badge Access - Front Desk Procedure

## Issuing a visitor badge

Ask the visitor for photo identification and check the name against the day's
expected-visitor list. Print a badge from the front desk terminal. Write the
host's name on the badge in the space provided. Hand the badge over and ask the
visitor to wear it above the waist and in view for the whole visit.

## Returning a badge

Visitors return badges at the front desk on their way out. Wipe the badge with
the cleaning cloth kept in the top drawer and put it back in the return tray.
Badges in the return tray are re-used the same day.

## If a badge is not returned

Mark the visitor's line on the expected-visitor list with an N. At the end of
the day, send the list of unreturned badges to the security office. Badges that
have been missing for 7 days are deactivated.

## Contractors

Contractors on a job lasting more than one day are issued a week badge instead
of a day badge. A week badge is valid for 7 days from the date printed on it.
Contractors sign the contractor register in addition to the visitor list.
