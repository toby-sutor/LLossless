# Hold Shelf Management

## Placing an item on the shelf

An item that is on loan or held at another branch can be reserved. When a requested item arrives, print a hold slip and fold it into the item so the name is visible from the spine, then shelve it alphabetically by the borrower's surname. Borrowers should bring their library card when they come to collect their item.

## How long an item waits

An item waits on the hold shelf for 7 days, counted from the day the borrower is notified by email that it is ready for collection, not the day the item arrived.

## Clearing the shelf

Sweep the shelf every morning before opening. Items past 7 days go back into circulation, or on to the next borrower in the queue if there is one.

## Borrowers who cannot collect in time

A borrower who asks before the 7 days are up can have the item held for a further 7 days. This is granted once per request.

## Items nobody collects twice

An item that has been on the hold shelf twice without being collected goes back to the shelves, the request is cancelled, and the borrower will need to make a new request if they still want it.
