# Hold Shelf Management

## Placing an item on the shelf

You can reserve an item that is on loan or held at another branch.

When a requested item arrives, print a hold slip and fold it into the item so the
name is visible from the spine.

Shelve items alphabetically by the borrower's surname, so borrowers find items on the hold shelf under their surname.

Bring your library card.

## How long an item waits

We email you when an item is ready to collect. It waits on the hold shelf for 7 days from the day we email you, rather than the day it arrived.

## Clearing the shelf

Sweep the shelf every morning before opening.

Items that are not collected within 7 days go back into circulation, or on to the next borrower in the queue if there is one.

## Borrowers who cannot collect in time

Borrowers who cannot collect within 7 days can ask before the 7 days are up to have an item held for a further 7 days.

This extension is granted once per request or reservation.

## Items nobody collects twice

After an item has been on the hold shelf twice without being collected for the same request or reservation, it goes back into circulation on the shelves. The request or reservation is cancelled, and the borrower must reserve the item again.
