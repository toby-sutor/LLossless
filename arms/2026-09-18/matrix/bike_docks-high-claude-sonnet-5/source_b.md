# Docking Station Faults - Support Desk

## What riders report

Riders call about bikes that will not release and bikes that will not lock. Both
usually mean a dock fault rather than a bike fault.

## Reading the fault code

Ask the rider to read the code on the dock display. E2 is a jammed locking pin
and E4 is a loss of power to the point.

## Escalating E2

Raise an E2 to the field crew. If the field crew have already tried 3 times,
raise it to the workshop instead.

## Escalating E4

Raise an E4 to the electrical contractor the same day.

## Refunds

A rider charged for a journey that ended at a faulted dock gets the journey
refunded. Refund at the time of the call; do not wait for the fault to be
confirmed.

## Taking a station out of service

The support desk takes a station out of service in the app when more than half
its docks are faulted.
