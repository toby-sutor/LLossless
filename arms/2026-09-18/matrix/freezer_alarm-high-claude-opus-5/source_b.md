# Cold Storage Alarm Checklist

## Reading the alarm

The alarm panel names the unit and shows the temperature that triggered it. Note
the unit name; there are three units on this floor and two of them alarm at the
same set point.

## Check the door seal

Run a finger along the seal. Ice on the seal holds the door open by a few
millimetres and is the usual cause of a slow rise.

## Escalating

Call the on-call technician if the temperature is still rising 20 minutes after
you arrive. Give the unit name, the reading you took on arrival and the reading
now.

## Recording

Every alarm gets a line in the freezer log whether or not anything was wrong. An
alarm with no line in the log is indistinguishable from an alarm nobody attended.

## Weekend cover

The night phone is carried by the weekend duty officer from 18:00 on Friday
until 08:00 on Monday.
