# Freezer Alarm Response - Night Shift

## What triggers an alarm

Each freezer alarms when the internal temperature rises above its set point for
more than 10 minutes. The alarm sounds locally and sends a message to the night
phone.

## First response

Go to the freezer and read the display. Write the reading and the time in the
freezer log before touching anything else. The reading at the moment of arrival
is the one the lab needs and it cannot be recovered later.

## If the door is ajar

Close the door and wait 20 minutes. If the temperature falls back below the set
point, log it as a door event and no further action is needed.

## If the temperature keeps rising

Call the on-call technician. While waiting, do not open the door. Opening a
door on a failing freezer to check the contents is the most common cause of loss
in this building.

## Moving contents

Contents are moved only on the technician's instruction and only into a freezer
that already has space at the same set point. Never split a single study's
samples across two freezers.
