# Loading Dock Booking - Standard Operating Procedure

## Booking a slot

All deliveries need a dock slot. Book slots through the facilities portal. A slot is 45 minutes. A vehicle over 7.5 tonnes needs two consecutive slots and the portal will not let you book them separately.

## Booking window

Slots open 14 days ahead and close 4 hours before the slot starts. There is no same-hour booking.

## Booking by phone

If the portal is down, call the dock booking line on extension 2140 and the dock supervisor will enter the slot for you. Phone requests are taken between 08:00 and 16:00 on working days only.

## Arriving

Drivers report to the gatehouse with the booking reference. The gatehouse checks the reference against the day sheet and directs the vehicle to a bay. A driver without a reference waits in the holding area until a slot is free.

## Overrunning

A vehicle still on the bay 15 minutes after its slot ends is logged as an overrun. Three overruns in a quarter and the supplier is asked to rebook through their account manager.
