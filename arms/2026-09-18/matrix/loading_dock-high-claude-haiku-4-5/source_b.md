# Dock Slot Requests

## Requesting a slot

Every delivery needs a dock slot. Request slots through the facilities portal. A
slot is 45 minutes long. Vehicles over 7.5 tonnes need two consecutive slots.

## When to request

Slots open 14 days ahead and close 4 hours before the slot starts.

## Requesting by phone

If the portal is down, call the dock booking line on extension 2140 and the dock
supervisor will enter the slot for you. Phone requests are taken between 08:00
and 16:00 on working days only.

## On the day

Drivers report to the gatehouse and give the booking reference. Drivers without
a reference wait in the holding area.

## Late finishes

A vehicle still on the bay 15 minutes after its slot ends is logged as an
overrun.
