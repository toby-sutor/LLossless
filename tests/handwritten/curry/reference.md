Vegetarian Thai Green Curry Receipt

Serves 4 | ~30 min

Ingredients

- 1 tbsp coconut oil or 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2-4 tbsp green curry paste (check it's veg - many contain shrimp paste)
- 1 can (400 ml) full-fat coconut milk
- 1 onion, sliced
- 2-3 cups mixed vegetables: bell pepper, green beans, baby corn, broccoli, mushrooms, eggplant, zucchini
- 200-400 g firm tofu, cubed (optional)
- 1 tsp palm or brown sugar
- 1-2 tbsp soy sauce or 1 tsp salt
- Juice of ½ lime
- Handful Thai basil (or regular basil)
- 4–6 kaffir lime leaves, lightly crushed
- Jasmine rice, to serve

Method
1. Press & fry the tofu. Press excess water from the tofu, cut into cubes, and pan-fry in a little oil until golden on all sides. Set aside.
2. Spoon the thick cream from the top of the coconut milk into a hot wok with the oil. Cook until it bubbles and the oil separates, ~2 min.
3. Add the curry paste and fry 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Add onion and the remaining coconut milk. Bring to a gentle simmer - don't boil hard.
5. Add the harder vegetables first (beans, broccoli, eggplant, zucchini), simmer 5 min, then the softer ones (pepper, mushrooms) and the tofu. Simmer another 5 min until tender but still crisp.
6. Season with sugar, soy sauce and lime juice. Taste and adjust seasoning with salt or more soy sauce - it should be salty, slightly sweet, and sour.
7. Stir in the basil off the heat. Serve over jasmine rice with extra basil and a wedge of lime.

Tip: Add a splash of water if it gets too thick or use a splash of vegetable stock for a richer curry.
