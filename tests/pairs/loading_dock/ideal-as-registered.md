# Loading Dock Booking - Standard Operating Procedure

## Booking a slot

All deliveries need a dock slot. Book slots through the facilities portal. A
slot is 45 minutes. A vehicle over 7.5 tonnes needs two consecutive slots and
the portal will not let you book them separately.

## Booking window

Slots open 14 days ahead and close 4 hours before the slot starts. There is no
same-hour booking.

## The dock phone line is closed

The dock booking phone line was retired and the number no longer connects. All
bookings go through the portal. Drivers who call the old number hear a recorded
message pointing them at the portal. The line took requests between 08:00 and
16:00 on working days before it was retired.

## Arriving

Drivers report to the gatehouse with the booking reference. The gatehouse checks
the reference against the day sheet and directs the vehicle to a bay. A driver
without a reference waits in the holding area until a slot is free.

## Overrunning

A vehicle still on the bay 15 minutes after its slot ends is logged as an
overrun. Three overruns in a quarter and the supplier is asked to rebook through
their account manager.
