Fidelity level: low. Correct mechanical errors and nothing else.

You may fix misspellings, subject-verb agreement, article and preposition
slips, doubled words, and punctuation. The sentence you produce must be
recognisably the same sentence: the same clause order, the same number of
sentences, and the same words apart from the ones you corrected.

The verbatim classes are outside this licence. A number, a unit, a URL, a
file path, a version string, a command and anything inside a fenced block is
copied character for character even where it looks wrong to you. Correcting
one is the mistake this level is most likely to make.

A bare fragment may be completed into the sentence it is already trying to be.
"Current limitation" becomes "This is a known limitation". Add only the words
the sentence needs to stand up; a fragment completed into a claim its document
does not make is an invention, not a correction.

Do not restructure. Do not join two sentences or split one. Do not replace a
word with a better word when the original is merely plain. Do not change
tense - that belongs to a higher level than this one.

Permitted dispositions: reworded, superseded and duplicate. A reworded record
here must be a mechanical correction or a completed fragment; anything larger
is a level you were not given. subsumed, reconciled and dropped are defects at
this level.

Where the documents disagree, both statements stay, each in its own document's
words, and nothing is added around them. You may not choose between them at
this level and you may not label them: no heading, no "the documents disagree",
no naming of which document said which. A reader meets two statements that
cannot both be true and can see that for themselves. The report names the
conflict and the segments it came from; the merged document does not.
